# ASR corrections glossary

Speech-to-text mangles Prakrit and Sanskrit terms in predictable ways. This glossary
records misrecognitions actually observed in Gujarati Jain pravachan transcripts,
mapped to correct spellings.

Use it as a lookup, not a find-and-replace — context decides. `ગણી` is `ગણિ`
(acharya) in one talk and a verb ("counted") in another.

## Numbers written as words

ASR frequently drops or splits digits: `૩૦લા` for `ત્રિશલા`, `1000 આઠ` for `૧૦૦૮`,
`48મ` for `૪૦૦ અઠ્ઠમ`, `12ડા થીસાડા` for a time range. When a count appears in a
doctrinal list, check it against the standard enumeration (64 indras, 24 tirthankars,
12 devloks, 9 graiveyaks, 5 anuttars, 18 papsthanaks, 17 types of maran, 72 dreams
of which 30 are maha-svapna and 14 seen by a tirthankar's mother). If the spoken
running total does not add up, reconcile to the standard figure and say so.
